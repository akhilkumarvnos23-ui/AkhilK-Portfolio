## Project Overview

Over 700 million people worldwide experience dyslexia, yet most digital learning platforms are designed primarily for average readers rather than learners with reading difficulties.

Students often struggle with

- Dense text
- Poor readability
- Lack of personalization
- Limited accessibility options
- Low motivation during learning

The goal was to design a learning experience that improves reading accessibility while increasing confidence and engagement.

**Project Details:**
- **Duration**: 3.5 Months
- **Role**: UI/UX Designer
- **Tools**: Figma, FigJam, Google Forms

## About the Product

Inclusive Aide is an accessibility-first mobile learning platform designed to help students with dyslexia learn more effectively through personalized and inclusive educational experiences. The platform combines dyslexia-friendly reading tools, text-to-speech, customizable reading settings, progress tracking, and gamified activities to reduce cognitive overload and improve engagement. By connecting students, teachers, and parents through dedicated dashboards, Inclusive Aide fosters collaboration, supports individualized learning, and empowers students to build confidence while making education more accessible for everyone.

## Problem Statement

Students with dyslexia often face significant challenges when using digital learning platforms because most educational tools are designed for average readers and lack accessibility features such as dyslexia-friendly fonts, text-to-speech, personalized reading settings, and clear progress tracking. These limitations increase cognitive load, reduce comprehension and engagement, and negatively impact learners' confidence. There is a need for an inclusive learning platform that adapts to individual reading needs, supports teachers and parents, and creates a more accessible, engaging, and confidence-building educational experience.

## Research

# Literature Review

I reviewed three research papers focused on assistive learning technologies.

The research consistently highlighted

- Text-to-Speech improves comprehension.
- Reading customization reduces cognitive load.
- Motivation significantly impacts learning outcomes.
- Long-term success depends on support from teachers and parents.

# Opportunity

- Accessibility

- Parent Support
- Teacher Collaboration
- Gamification

into one unified ecosystem


### User Research 

I interviewed with:

- Students
- Teachers
- Parents 

# Key Findings

1. Students became mentally exhausted when reading long paragraphs.

2. Users wanted complete control over

Font
Line spacing
Background colors
Reading modes

3. Parents wanted better visibility into progress.

4. Teachers struggled to manage accessibility accommodations.

5. Gamification increased motivation.


### Design Opportunity
Research revealed five opportunities.

**Reduce Cognitive Load**
Simplify layouts and reduce visual clutter.

**Improve Readability**
Allow users to customize reading preferences.

**Increase Accessibility**
Provide Text-to-Speech and highlighted reading.

**Improve Motivation**
Introduce rewards and achievement systems.

**Support Collaboration**
Connect students, teachers, and parents.

## Solution

Inclusive Aide is a mobile-first learning platform that adapts content for students with dyslexia using personalized accessibility settings, AI-supported reading assistance, and collaborative progress tracking.

## Usability Testing

I tested the prototype with three participants.

**Tasks**
- Navigate to Reading
- Enable Text-to-Speech
- Customize Reading Settings


## Key Findings

Users appreciated

- Reading customization
- Simple navigation
- Progress tracking

However, testing revealed three usability issues

- "Add File" was difficult to discover.
- TTS button lacked visibility.
- Labels required better clarity.


## Iterations

Following testing I improved

**Navigation**

Added clearer labels.

**Teacher Dashboard**

Made primary actions easier to find.

**TTS**

Increased button size and repositioned controls.

**Reading Settings**

Organized options into grouped sections.


## Impact

- Although this was a concept project, usability testing showed that users could successfully complete the primary reading tasks after the design refinements.
- The solution demonstrated how accessibility can be embedded into the learning experience rather than treated as an afterthought.
