## About the Product

Commercial real estate acquisitions require analysts to review thousands of pages of leases, financial statements, rent rolls, and legal documents before making investment decisions.
These manual workflows are slow, repetitive, and susceptible to human error.
AriesView leverages AI to extract structured information from property documents, surface key insights, and streamline due diligence while keeping analysts in control of the final review.


## Problem Statement

Real estate analysts spent significant time navigating complex datasets and validating AI-generated outputs across multiple disconnected workflows. Large document sets and dense relational data increased cognitive load, making it difficult to identify critical information efficiently.
The objective was to simplify these workflows through intuitive interfaces that improved clarity, reduced cognitive effort, and supported faster decision-making.

## UX Challenges

Some of the primary challenges included:

- **Large Data Relationships**: Users needed to understand relationships across tens of thousands of interconnected data points.
- **AI Transparency**: Users required confidence in AI-generated insights and needed interfaces that made complex information easier to interpret.
- **Scalability**: Interfaces needed to support large enterprise datasets without overwhelming users.

**Solution:** Several design improvements were introduced:

- **Simplified Data Visualization:** Complex relationship structures were reorganized into cleaner visual patterns that reduced cognitive effort while maintaining analytical depth.
- **Improved Navigation:** Navigation patterns were redesigned to minimize unnecessary clicks and provide clearer movement between related information.
- **Interactive Prototypes:** High-fidelity prototypes were created in Figma and ProtoPie to validate interaction behavior before development.
- **Design System Expansion:** Additional reusable components were introduced to improve consistency across the application.
